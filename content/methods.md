title: Methods
---

Every review here is made the same way. Claude reads the whole paper — text, figures,
tables and, where it matters, the supplementary files — plus anything outside the paper
that bears on it: the group's earlier work, funding and conflicts of interest, company
statements, published commentary. It then writes the review against the guide below.
The editor reads it before it goes up.

The guide and the instructions the model follows are public and carry one version number, **{{GUIDE_VERSION}}**. Every review says which
version it was written under. Changes are listed at the end of the guide.
